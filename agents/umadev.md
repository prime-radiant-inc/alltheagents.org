Orchestrates a 9-seat dev team (8 specialist roles + coordinator) over borrowed AI brains — driving one of 5 existing AI coding CLIs rather than owning any model endpoint. Deterministic acceptance floor (build/test/contract verification runs regardless of model self-assessment). 113 governance checks with fail-open design. Delivery evidence with proof packs, scorecards, compliance mappings (SOC 2 / ISO 27001 / EU AI Act). Spec-driven with 34 normative clauses. Local-first retrieval with BM25 + optional local vector embeddings. Frontend↔backend contract verification.
