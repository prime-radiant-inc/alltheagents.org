8090 targets organizations where software changes require audit trails — healthcare payers, financial institutions, federal agencies — and where simply letting an agent edit code is unacceptable without oversight. Its Software Factory product is an AI-native SDLC control plane: business intent is stated in plain English, human teams and AI agents coordinate execution, and every decision leaves an audit trail, while the Enterprise offering has 8090 itself build and host purpose-built applications. The CMS engagement converted 18M+ lines of Medicare COBOL and Assembly claims code into over 100,000 plain-English business rules, and BISSELL automated part-approval workflows with roughly 94% auto-approval. Buyers are enterprises and government agencies; the founder is Chamath Palihapitiya and the Series A was led by Salesforce Ventures.
