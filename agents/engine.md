Engine came out of cto.new with the thesis that buyers want outcomes delivered by a division of labor, not a single chatbot: users hire a ready-made team from a marketplace (Engineering, Marketing Agency, Website Creation) or list their own. A technical-lead agent decomposes the request and delegates to backend, frontend, and QA engineer agents running in fast, secure cloud sandboxes, with model routing across Claude, OpenAI, Gemini, Mistral, Grok, and DeepSeek under an Auto mode. MCP servers and built-in integrations (Linear, Sentry, Vercel, Notion) extend what the agents can reach, and the platform also handles adjacent work like email and scheduling. The free tier is ad-supported with daily-reset limits, which positions it as an on-ramp for founders and non-engineers delegating software work.
