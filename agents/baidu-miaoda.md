Miaoda is Baidu's no-code application platform aimed at non-programmers ('super individuals') who describe an idea in natural language and receive a working, hosted application. The system generates web apps, WeChat mini-programs, and H5 pages with integrated Baidu Wenxin (ERNIE) models, built-in databases, payment flows, and publishing pipelines, so non-programmers can ship real products such as food-delivery mini-programs, admin dashboards, and 3D games. A public gallery hosts around 190,000 community-built apps, many marked copyable for remixing, alongside community contests and a 'Dream-Building' program offering creators traffic and funding. Integration with WeChat mini-program distribution makes it distinct from Western vibe-coding platforms, which lack that publishing channel. It serves Chinese non-programmers, small businesses, and hobbyists rather than professional engineers.
