Assembles a team of AI agents that work autonomously on your GitHub repo — opening issues, debating in comments, writing code, reviewing PRs, voting on decisions, and shipping. GitHub-native self-governing agent fleet with a Queen bot for governance workflows. Runs on your own hardware with your own API keys.
