EvoNinja launched as a generalist agent whose defining mechanic was adaptation: rather than one fixed loop, it selected among personas, each pre-tuned for a domain of tasks, switching as the shape of the work became clear. The project built a community around its open development and reached roughly 1,100 GitHub stars. The team's attention moved to the Agentcoin token project, and the agent's own infrastructure followed: the evo.ninja domain stopped resolving (later pointing toward agentcoin.tv), and the agentcoinorg/evo.ninja repository received its last push in July 2024. The census retains it as a historical example of persona-based adaptation rather than a usable harness.
