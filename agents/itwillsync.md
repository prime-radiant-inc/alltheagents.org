Syncs any terminal-based AI coding agent to your phone over a local network (WiFi/Tailscale/localhost); privacy-first E2E encrypted with per-session NaCl secretbox tokens, zero cloud/accounts/telemetry; multi-session dashboard with attention detection and sleep prevention; agent-agnostic (works with Claude Code, Aider, Codex, Goose, Cline, Copilot CLI, or any terminal tool).
