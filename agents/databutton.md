Databutton let non-developers describe an application in natural language and iterate on it with an AI agent that generated and modified the underlying code, with one-click deployment to databutton.app subdomains or custom domains. The service targeted founders and business users, and it differentiated itself by bundling access to human experts alongside the agent for users who outgrew autonomous iteration. The platform operated on a subscription model. In 2026 the company pivoted: the databutton.com domain now serves Riff, a Norwegian enterprise-agent company for supply-chain operations, and the consumer app builder itself is no longer offered under that name.
