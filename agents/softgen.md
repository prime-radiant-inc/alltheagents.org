Softgen targets founders and operators who need a working SaaS-style product rather than a prototype: the user describes an application, the system asks clarifying questions, produces a development plan, then builds a Next.js application with a managed Supabase database, auth, and payments. Iteration happens in a live preview, and finished apps deploy to Vercel under the user's own account with GitHub export, so the code, database, and hosting remain portable and commercially owned after cancellation. One-click integrations cover analytics, marketing pixels, and support widgets. Paid plans start at $25 per month with credit-based usage and 12+ selectable models. It competes with Lovable, Bolt.new, and Replit on model choice and ownership terms.
