Tencent Yuanqi (腾讯元器) is Tencent's consumer-facing platform for creating and hosting AI agents without code, aimed squarely at the WeChat ecosystem. Builders assemble agents from prompts and knowledge bases, then publish them as WeChat Official Account assistants that answer reader questions using article history, as customer-service bots handling 7x24 inquiries, as 'IP avatar' persona clones that speak in a blogger's voice, or as brand utilities spanning legal advice to PPT generation. The underlying models are Tencent's Hunyuan with DeepSeek available for some agents, and a marketplace showcases recently updated agents across categories like expert advisors, IP avatars, and games. It is not a coding tool: there is no mechanism for generating or modifying software, so it sits outside the coding-agent definition. The census carries it because earlier sweeps captured it as an 'agent platform'; its audience is WeChat publishers and brands, not developers building software.
