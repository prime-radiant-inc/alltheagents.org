PlusB is a forward-deployed engineering firm that builds and operates custom LLM systems inside client infrastructure rather than selling a product anyone can install. Engagements follow an audit-scope-deploy-maintain arc: engineers train or adapt models for a client's domain, deploy into their cloud VPC or on-premise cluster, and maintain the system around the clock with a zero-data-retention guarantee. Six deployment patterns cover voice AI with sub-100ms latency, autonomous database-and-workflow agents, document OCR for invoices and medical charts, domain-specific translation, knowledge-base-only private chat, and AI governance with audit logging. The firm targets regulated, data-sensitive industries — healthcare, education, agriculture, enterprise — where sending data to a hosted API is not an option. Its research arm publishes small-language-model work and open weights on Hugging Face.
