LLM4Mol maps the 2022-2023 wave of research applying large language models to molecular and life sciences, giving researchers a structured entry point by domain rather than a flat paper dump. Each section lists citations with links to the underlying arXiv papers and released code, which made it a practical starting point for researchers deciding where LLM methods might apply to their chemistry or protein work. Entries stop around mid-2023 and the repository has not been meaningfully updated since, so it now serves as a historical snapshot of the field rather than a current survey.
