A synthetic dataset of 5,000 records capturing behavioral and productivity patterns of AI developers (focus hours, burnout indicators, task completion rates) generated by Syncora.ai, intended for ML training, productivity forecasting, and burnout detection research. Provides high-fidelity, privacy-safe synthetic data that simulates realistic developer workflows without the ethical concerns or privacy liabilities of real employee surveillance data. NOTE: This is a dataset, not a coding agent harness — most agent-feature fields are not applicable.
