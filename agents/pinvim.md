The most minimal AI coding agent for Neovim, designed to embrace the simplicity of the CLI rather than imitating complex IDE features. Context-aware (sends buffer, cwd, selection, and optional diagnostics), unsaved-buffer aware (treats Neovim buffer content as the source of truth over stale disk files), and stays out of the user's way.
