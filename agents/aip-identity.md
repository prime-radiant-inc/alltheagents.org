Agent Identity Protocol (AIP) — decentralized cryptographic identity (Ed25519 keypairs), verifiable trust chains (vouching), and E2E encrypted messaging for AI agents. Enables secure, verifiable agent-to-agent communication without a central authority. Integrates with MCP to sign MCP requests and fill the 'agent identity gap.'
