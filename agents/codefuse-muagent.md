Multi-agent framework driven by a Knowledge Graph (Eventic Knowledge Graph / EKG) engine rather than purely model-based or fixed-flow approaches. Successful exploration paths are documented into the KG to reduce future token costs. Supports MultiAgent, FunctionCall, CodeInterpreter, and RAG. Visual drag-and-drop canvas for building agent workflows. Validated in complex DevOps scenarios at Ant Group.
