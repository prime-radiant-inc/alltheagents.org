Magic pursues software-engineering automation at the model layer: frontier pre-training combined with domain-specific reinforcement learning, ultra-long context windows, and inference-time compute, on the thesis that whole-repository context is what makes automated software engineering tractable. The company has raised $515 million from investors including Nat Friedman, Daniel Gross, CapitalG, Sequoia, and Eric Schmidt, and operates a Google Cloud partnership for compute. As of its latest site revision it publishes research updates and hires aggressively but offers no public coding-agent product, though a product-track engineering role suggests one is planned. For this census it matters as model infrastructure that other harnesses may build on, not as a harness itself.
