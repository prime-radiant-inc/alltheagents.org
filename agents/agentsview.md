Local-first analytics tool (single Go binary) that browses, searches, and tracks costs across 20+ AI coding agents' session data. SQLite-indexed for 100x faster queries vs re-parsing raw files. Multi-backend (SQLite, PostgreSQL, DuckDB), full-text + semantic search, SSE live updates, privacy-focused (loopback binding, disableable telemetry), Tauri desktop wrapper.
