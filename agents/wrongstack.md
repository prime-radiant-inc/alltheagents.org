A complete, from-scratch agent (not a wrapper or orchestration kit) with its own compact kernel (~1,670 lines), 61 built-in tools, 77-role multi-agent fleet, SAGE persistent memory (SQLite/FTS5), inter-agent mailbox, Chimera auto-review, 6 surfaces (REPL, TUI, WebUI, SimpleUI, Desktop, HQ), all working standalone with no third-party CLI dependency. --no-features runs fully offline.
