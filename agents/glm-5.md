Series of large language models (GLM-5/5.1/5.2) targeting complex systems engineering and long-horizon agentic tasks. GLM-5.2 delivers a solid 1M-token context, 744B params (40B active), IndexShare architecture reducing per-token FLOPs 2.9x at 1M context, and DeepSeek Sparse Attention. Best-in-class open-source coding/agentic performance.
