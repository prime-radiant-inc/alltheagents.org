TMPA (Textual Multi-Agent Process Architecture) — vendor-neutral governance architecture for long-running work performed by AI agents and humans. Treats AI agent work as durable governance objects rather than facts trapped in chat/model sessions. Reconstructs lifecycle, authority, conflicts, and audit state from inspectable text evidence. Four-role fixed team (PM/DEV/OPS/QA) with EVAL observing independently.
