Lindy occupies the AI-teammate niche: instead of a coding loop, it runs ongoing business workflows — email triage, meeting notes and transcription, CRM updates, report generation, scheduled routines — from inside Slack, iMessage, Gmail, and a Chrome extension. Approval gates block irreversible actions without user confirmation, memory lives in editable plain files, and learned skills accumulate per user. It connects to over a thousand tools and any MCP server, joins meetings as a participant, and carries SOC 2 Type II, GDPR, and HIPAA compliance for regulated customers. Teams adopt it for operations and GTM automation at $29.99-$199.99 per user per month with credit-based usage.
